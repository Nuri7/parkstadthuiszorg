# Parkstad Thuiszorg – User Personas

**5 realistic user personas** for people who would visit and use [Parkstad Thuiszorg](https://nuri7.github.io/parkstadthuiszorg/).

The website focuses on **een vertrouwd gezicht, een gerust gevoel** in the Parkstad region (Kerkrade, Heerlen, Landgraaf, Brunssum, etc.). These personas represent the main target groups: elderly living alone, post-hospital recovery patients, overburdened family caregivers, aging couples, and younger people with chronic conditions who want to stay independent at home.

---

## 1. Maria “Mien” Schrijvers
**Age:** 78  
**Location:** Kerkrade  
**Role:** Widow living alone

**Background**  
Retired shop assistant, lives in her small terraced house where she raised her children.

**Situation**  
Arthritis, recent minor fall, mild forgetfulness. Struggles with showering, dressing, cooking, and shopping.

**Needs**  
- Personal care (verzorging)  
- Light household help  
- Medication reminders  
- Someone to chat with in the local Limburg dialect

**Goals**  
Stay in her own home as long as possible and avoid a nursing home.

**Pain Points**  
Feels lonely, distrusts “big care organizations,” wants caregivers who really listen.

**Website Behavior**  
Visits the site on her daughter’s tablet after seeing the flyer. Mainly wants to read the “About Us” page and call for a free intake conversation.

## 2. Hans Müller
**Age:** 69  
**Location:** Heerlen  
**Role:** Recently retired, lives with wife

**Background**  
Ex-construction worker, just had knee-replacement surgery.

**Situation**  
Temporary need for intensive support during recovery; wife has her own back problems.

**Needs**  
- Wound care  
- Mobility assistance  
- Physiotherapy coordination  
- Help with daily activities (6–8 weeks)

**Goals**  
Recover quickly at home so he can get back to his vegetable garden and cycling club.

**Pain Points**  
Afraid of becoming dependent; hates hospital readmissions.

**Website Behavior**  
Searches Google for “thuiszorg na operatie Parkstad,” lands on the site, reads the services page, and fills in the contact form himself.

---

## 3. Sophie Leclerc
**Age:** 52  
**Location:** Landgraaf  
**Role:** Daughter & informal caregiver (mantelzorger)

**Background**  
Works part-time as a teacher, mother of two teenagers.

**Situation**  
Her 84-year-old mother has early dementia and lives alone nearby. Sophie is exhausted from daily visits.

**Needs**  
- Daytime personal care, meal preparation, medication supervision  
- Occasional respite care

**Goals**  
Keep her mother safe and happy at home while protecting her own health and job.

**Pain Points**  
Feels guilty asking for help; overwhelmed by WMO/PGB paperwork.

**Website Behavior**  
Found the site via Facebook group “Zorg Parkstad.” Spends time on the “services” and “about us” pages, then books an intake meeting for herself and her mother.

---

## 4. Theo & Carla Janssen
**Age:** 81 & 79  
**Location:** Brunssum  
**Role:** Married couple, both need support

**Background**  
Theo (former miner) has advanced COPD and mobility issues; Carla is his main caregiver but tiring fast.

**Situation**  
They want to stay together in their own house.

**Needs**  
- Combined personal care for Theo  
- Household help, night support  
- Relief for Carla (mantelzorgondersteuning)

**Goals**  
Grow old together at home without one of them having to move to a care facility.

**Pain Points**  
Carla’s exhaustion and Theo’s fear of “being a burden.”

**Website Behavior**  
Their son shows them the site on a laptop. They prefer calling the phone number because they value personal contact.

---

## 5. Lieke van de Berg
**Age:** 45  
**Location:** Voerendaal  
**Role:** Person with chronic condition, lives alone

**Background**  
Diagnosed with Multiple Sclerosis 12 years ago, works part-time from home as a graphic designer.

**Situation**  
Uses a wheelchair, needs help with transfers, hygiene, and household tasks.

**Needs**  
- Flexible personal assistance (PGB)  
- Reliable caregivers who respect her independence and privacy

**Goals**  
Keep full control over her life and continue working from home.

**Pain Points**  
Tired of caregivers who treat her like a “patient” instead of an adult; values professionalism and flexibility.

**Website Behavior**  
Very digitally savvy. Reads the entire site, checks the team page for the “personal with heart” vibe, then sends a message via the contact form requesting a meeting.
